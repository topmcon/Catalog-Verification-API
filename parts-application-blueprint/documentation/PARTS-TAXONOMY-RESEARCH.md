## PARTS INDUSTRY STRUCTURE

### Level 1: DEPARTMENTS (Top-Level)

Based on industry standards from major parts distributors:

1. **Appliance Parts** - Parts for major and small appliances
2. **HVAC Parts** - Heating, ventilation, air conditioning components
3. **Electronics Parts** - Electronic components and modules
4. **Plumbing Parts** - Water system components
5. **Electrical Parts** - Electrical components and supplies
6. **OEM Parts** - Original Equipment Manufacturer parts
7. **Universal Parts** - Cross-compatible generic parts

## PART ATTRIBUTES (Top 15 Filter Attributes by Category)

### Example: Refrigerator Parts > Compressor

1. **Compatible Brand** (Whirlpool, GE, Samsung, LG, etc.)
2. **Compatible Model Series** (Model number prefixes)
3. **Part Type** (Compressor)
4. **Compressor Type** (Reciprocating, Rotary, Scroll)
5. **Refrigerant Type** (R134a, R600a, R410A)
6. **Cooling Capacity (BTU)** (Number)
7. **Voltage** (115V, 220V, etc.)
8. **Amperage** (Number)
9. **Mounting Type** (Vertical, Horizontal)
10. **Connection Type** (Flare, Solder)
11. **Oil Type** (POE, Mineral, Synthetic)
12. **OEM/Aftermarket** (Original, Compatible, Universal)
13. **Warranty** (90 days, 1 year, etc.)
14. **Condition** (New, Refurbished)
15. **Part Number** (Manufacturer part number)

### Example: HVAC Parts > Air Filter

1. **Filter Size** (20x25x4, 16x20x1, etc.)
2. **MERV Rating** (1-16)
3. **Filter Type** (Pleated, Electrostatic, HEPA, Carbon)
4. **Filter Material** (Fiberglass, Polyester, Synthetic)
5. **Frame Material** (Cardboard, Metal, Plastic)
6. **Nominal Thickness** (1", 2", 4")
7. **Actual Thickness** (Decimal inches)
8. **Nominal Width** (Inches)
9. **Nominal Height** (Inches)
10. **Replacement Interval** (1 month, 3 months, 6 months, 12 months)
11. **Airflow Resistance** (Low, Medium, High)
12. **Particle Capture Rate** (Percentage)
13. **Filter Application** (Residential, Commercial, Industrial)
14. **Compatible Systems** (Furnace, AC, Heat Pump)
15. **Pack Quantity** (1, 2, 4, 6)

### Example: Appliance Parts > Heating Element

1. **Compatible Brand**
2. **Compatible Models**
3. **Element Type** (Bake, Broil, Preheat, Convection)
4. **Wattage** (Number)
5. **Voltage** (120V, 240V)
6. **Element Shape** (Coil, Hidden, Straight)
7. **Bracket Included** (Yes/No)
8. **Terminal Type** (Screw, Push-on, Spade)
9. **Length** (Inches)
10. **Width** (Inches)
11. **OEM Part Number**
12. **Material** (Nichrome, Stainless Steel)
13. **Surface Type** (Sheathed, Exposed)
14. **Installation Difficulty** (Easy, Moderate, Expert)
15. **Warranty Period**

## CROSS-REFERENCE FIELDS

Parts have unique identification requirements:

### Critical Identifiers
- **Manufacturer Part Number** (Primary ID)
- **OEM Part Number** (Original part #)
- **Compatible Part Numbers** (Superseded, alternate part #s)
- **Compatible Models** (Which appliances/systems this fits)
- **Compatible Brands** (Which brands it works with)
- **Universal Fit** (True/False - fits multiple brands)
- **Supersedes Part Numbers** (Old part #s this replaces)
- **Superseded By** (New part # that replaces this)

---

## PHYSICAL SPECIFICATIONS

Parts require detailed physical measurements:

- **Length** (Decimal inches or mm)
- **Width** (Decimal inches or mm)
- **Height / Thickness** (Decimal inches or mm)
- **Diameter** (For circular parts)
- **Thread Size** (For threaded parts)
- **Weight** (Ounces or pounds)
- **Material** (Plastic, Metal, Stainless Steel, etc.)
- **Color** (If applicable)
- **Finish** (If applicable)

---

## ELECTRICAL SPECIFICATIONS

For electrical parts:

- **Voltage** (120V, 240V, 12V, 24V, etc.)
- **Amperage** (Amps)
- **Wattage** (Watts)
- **Frequency** (50Hz, 60Hz)
- **Phase** (Single, Three-Phase)
- **Horsepower** (For motors)
- **RPM** (Revolutions per minute)
- **Capacitance** (For capacitors - µF)
- **Resistance** (Ohms)

---

## COMPATIBILITY & FITMENT

Critical for parts:

- **Fits Brands** (List of compatible brands)
- **Fits Models** (Specific model numbers)
- **Model Series Compatibility** (Model prefixes)
- **Year Range** (Manufacturing years)
- **Direct Replacement For** (OEM part numbers)
- **Replaces Part Numbers** (List of superseded parts)
- **Installation Notes** (Special requirements)
- **Tools Required** (Screwdriver, Wrench, etc.)
